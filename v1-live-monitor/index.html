FAQ

CCTV in Dubai — your questions, answered

Is Artek approved to install CCTV in Dubai?

Yes. We are SIRA certified and Dubai Police approved, and we’ve been installing security systems in Dubai for 15+ years.

How much will my CCTV installation cost?

It depends on your property, the number of cameras and the cabling involved. That’s why we start with a free site survey and follow it with a written quote — you’ll know exactly what’s included before any work begins.

What happens during the free site survey?

We visit your villa, apartment, shop, office or warehouse, review the entry points and blind spots with you, and recommend where cameras should go. The survey is free and comes with a written quote.

What support do I get after installation?
